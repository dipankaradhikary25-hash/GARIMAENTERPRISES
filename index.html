Documents Ki Jankari (Zaroori Kagzat)

1. Jaati Praman Patra (Caste Certificate)
  • Aadhaar Card (Original)
  • Ration Card ya Bijli Bill
  • Pitaji ka Caste Certificate (Puraana)
  • Ek Passport Size Photo
  • Gram Pradhan/Parshad ka Goshna Patra
2. Aay Praman Patra (Income Certificate)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Salary Slip ya Bank Statement (Agar ho toh)
  • Swayam Goshna Patra (Self Declaration)
  • Ration Card ki copy
3. Naya PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card (Mobile Number Link hona zaroori hai)
  • 2 Fresh Passport Size Photo
  • Sahi Janm Tithi (DOB) ka proof
4. Ayushman Bharat Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card (Lal/Peela/Patra Grihasti)
  • PM Letter (Agar ho toh)
